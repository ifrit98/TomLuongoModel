# Public source map

The live harness uses FRED / Federal Reserve / OECD series where available. Relevant V2 additions include:

- DEXUSUK — U.S. dollars per British pound
- DEXSZUS — Swiss francs per U.S. dollar
- IRLTLT01FRM156N — France 10-year government yield proxy
- IRLTLT01CHM156N — Switzerland 10-year government yield proxy
- IR3TIB01JPM156N — Japan 3-month interbank proxy
- IR3TIB01CHM156N — Switzerland 3-month interbank proxy
- IR3TIB01USM156N — U.S. 3-month interbank proxy
- IR3TIB01GBM156N — U.K. 3-month interbank proxy; inspect freshness before use

Policy interpretation in the companion report also references the Swiss National Bank policy rate, Bank of Japan policy guidance, and the ECB Transmission Protection Instrument framework.

Public proxies are intentionally not treated as a substitute for professional FX basis, repo, CDS, dealer-flow, hedge and margin data.
